# Urban Sky - llms.txt

> Urban Sky is the global leader in tactical stratospheric balloon technology, providing persistent aerial surveillance, communications relay, and data collection solutions for defense, commercial, and scientific applications.

## Company Overview

Urban Sky, founded in 2016 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado, develops and manufactures reusable stratospheric microballoons that operate at altitudes up to 75,000 feet. The company has raised $30M in Series B funding and holds 20+ patents in stratospheric balloon technology.

## Key Products

### Microballoon Systems
- Reusable stratospheric balloons available in 10, 12, and 16 gore configurations
- Payload capacity: 4-50 lbs depending on configuration
- Float altitude: 61,000 - 75,000 feet
- Launch time: Under 5 minutes
- Multi-day flight duration

### Wallabee Payload Platform
- Modular, interchangeable sensor payloads
- Supports EO/IR cameras, LWIR thermal imaging, communications relay equipment
- 10cm resolution optical imagery capability
- Real-time data streaming

### Software Platform
- Mission planning and flight control
- Real-time tracking and telemetry
- Automated data processing
- Fleet management capabilities
